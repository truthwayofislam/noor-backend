A thoughtful approach

Encouragement, without the noise.

Noor is made to support personal practice and learning. Use the tools that help you, at your own pace.

The app is a companion for learning and daily routines; prayer calculations and available features may depend on your settings and device.

Account details such as email, username, and country are used for sign-in and your profile.
Progress and activity support your profile, rewards, and leaderboard features.
Passwords are hashed by the backend and are not stored as plain text.
Backend data is stored using the service's Turso database.
